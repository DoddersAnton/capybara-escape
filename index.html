🐹 Ezra's Capybara Escape Game

Please play to start
📱 Tip: turn your phone sideways for a bigger game

Guide the capybara through five levels: sneak out of the zoo, dash across a busy road, cross a quiet field, climb a snowy mountain, then duel Darth Vader for the escape pod. Move with WASD or Arrow Keys — on a phone or tablet, use the on-screen controls at the bottom (you can hide them). Duck into bushes, parked cars, tall grass or snowdrifts to hide — they block a watcher's line of sight. Press Space to swing your lightsaber — it does 1 damage to people (6 health), 2 damage to reindeer (4 health) and 2 damage to cars (10 health), so you can smash your way through traffic. Press E to spin 360° — a whirlwind that strikes everything around you (short cooldown). Hold B to block: your saber bounces cars and Vader's blows, but it slows you down and drains your Guard bar. Vader hits for 1 damage and you have 7 health, which regenerates while you stay out of his reach. Look out for glowing ⚡ speed pickups on each level — grab one for a few seconds of double speed (any left carries into the next level). You have 3 lives — get caught three times and it's back to the start!
